Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Introduction

Rehman Bakery respects your privacy and is committed to handling your personal information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you visit our website.

2. Information We Collect

You may choose to contact us using the contact details displayed on our website. If you contact us by email, we may receive personal information such as your name, email address and the contents of your message.

Our current website contact form does not submit or store enquiries. Information entered into the form is not automatically sent to Rehman Bakery.

3. How We Use Your Information

If you contact us directly, we may use the information you provide to respond to your enquiry, discuss products or services, and communicate with you about an order.

4. Sharing Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We will only share information where necessary to provide a service, comply with legal obligations or protect our legitimate interests.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

6. Cookies

Cookies are small files that websites may store on your device. This policy does not assume that our website currently uses cookies. If cookies or other tracking technologies are introduced, this policy and any required consent arrangements will be updated.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Under UK data protection law, you may have rights to access, correct or request the deletion of your personal information. You may also have the right to object to certain uses of your information.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or legal obligations change. Any updates will be published on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your personal information is handled, please contact us:

Email: rehman@rehmanbakery.co.uk
