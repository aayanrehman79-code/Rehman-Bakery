Oreo Cookie Recipe

Recipe Information

Serves: As many as you like!

Preparation Time: 32 minutes

Cooking Time: 15-18 minutes

Ingredients

For the Oreo Cookie

    Preparation Instructions

    1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan).
    2. Cream together the softened butter, light brown sugar and caster sugar until smooth.
    3. Add the egg and vanilla extract, mixing until fully combined.
    4. Gradually incorporate the flour, bicarbonate of soda and salt.
    5. Fold in the crushed Oreo biscuits and milk chocolate chunks.
    6. Divide the mixture into eight equal portions and shape into cookie dough balls.
    7. Place onto a lined baking tray, leaving space between each cookie.
    8. Bake for 12–15 minutes until the edges are lightly golden.
    9. Allow the cookies to cool before serving.

    Serving Suggestions

    Serve on a premium ceramic dessert plate with warm chocolate fudge sauce, crushed Oreo pieces and a scoop of vanilla ice cream.

Lotus Biscoff Cookie Recipe

Recipe Information

Servings: As many as you like!

Preparation Time: 25 minutes

Cooking Time: 12–15 minutes

Difficulty: Easy

Ingredients

For the Lotus Biscoff Cookie

    Preparation Instructions

    1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan).
    2. Cream together the softened butter, light brown sugar and caster sugar until smooth.
    3. Add the egg and vanilla extract, mixing until fully combined.
    4. Gradually add the plain flour, bicarbonate of soda and salt.
    5. Fold in the crushed Lotus Biscoff biscuits and white chocolate chunks.
    6. Divide the dough into eight equal portions.
    7. Flatten each portion and place one teaspoon of Lotus Biscoff spread in the centre.
    8. Wrap the dough around the filling and seal completely.
    9. Place onto a lined baking tray, leaving space between each cookie.
    10. Bake for 12–15 minutes until lightly golden around the edges.
    11. Allow the cookies to cool slightly before serving.

    Serving Suggestions

    Serve on a premium ceramic dessert plate with warmed Lotus Biscoff spread, crushed Biscoff biscuit crumbs and a scoop of vanilla ice cream.
